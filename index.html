How to Use

Getting started

  1. Settings tab: enter your company name, the last 4 digits of the bank account, and the beginning balance.
  2. Accounts & Payees tab: set up expense accounts (Maint., Fuel…), income accounts, and payee addresses.
  3. Write Check: fill in who you paid, the amount, and pick the expense account, then click Record. To split one check across several accounts, click + Add Line.
  4. Make Deposit: same idea for money coming in.
  5. Register: click any transaction to edit it. Click its Rec. box to mark it c (cleared) when it shows on your bank statement.
  6. Reconcile… (once a month): enter the statement’s ending date and balance, tick every item on the statement until the difference is $0.00, then click Finish. Those items are locked and show R in the register.

Windows

  • Double-click a desktop icon to open it.
  • Drag the title bar to move a window. Drag any edge or corner to resize it.
  • – Minimize sends the window to the taskbar at the bottom. Click it there to bring it back.
  • □ Maximize fills the screen (or double-click the title bar). Click again to restore.
  • ✕ Close closes the window. Your checkbook data is kept; double-click the icon to reopen.
  • The button at the far right of the taskbar shows the desktop.
